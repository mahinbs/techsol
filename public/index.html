⚙︎ Connections — mailbox, WhatsApp, Zoho
Set up once by whoever administers this installation. Credentials are stored on this machine only and are never sent anywhere except the service they belong to.

Mail intake (WF1)

Enquiries are read from one nominated mailbox. Only subjects matching the filter are read — nothing else in the mailbox is touched, and mail is never deleted.

WhatsApp intake (WF1)

Kapso is polled for inbound messages on one nominated business number — no webhook and no public address needed. Optional keyword filter; nothing is deleted at the provider.

Zoho connection

MOCK records nothing to Zoho. LIVE defaults to the sandbox CRM and the AI TEST Books org; connecting to production is refused unless you explicitly confirm written go-live approval.
